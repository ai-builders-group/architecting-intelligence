# Retrieval relevance score calibration (lst-9.18)

Trains logistic or isotonic calibration on labeled similarities and converts individual or batched scores into relevance probabilities.

- **Chapter:** 09
- **Class:** Runs on its own
- **Needs:** Python 3.12+; `numpy`, `pydantic`, `scikit-learn` (`pip install -r requirements/core.txt`)
- **Run:** `python chapters/ch09/lst-9.18.py`
